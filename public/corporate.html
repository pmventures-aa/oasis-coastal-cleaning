What a visit includes

The work, plainly

  • Desks, tables and shared surfaces wiped and reset
  • Restrooms cleaned and paper goods restocked from your supply
  • Break room — sink, microwave front, refrigerator exterior
  • Interior glass, entry doors and reception
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped, trash and recycling out
  • High-touch points: handles, switches, shared equipment

Who this is for

Suites, not stadiums

  • Professional offices and small firms
  • Medical, dental and wellness suites that need a quiet, documented clean
  • Shared workspaces and reception-led buildings
  • Teams that want one named person, not a rotating crew
  • Buildings that ask for a certificate of insurance on file

If the space is larger than one person can do well, we will say so on the quote rather than stretch it.

What property managers actually ask for

  • Certificate of insurance forwarded directly to the management office
