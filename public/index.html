Before you ask

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted individually, so there is no price list to point you at. What moves the number is square footage, how often we come, whether there are pets, and the condition on day one. Tell us about the space and a real number comes back within one business day — usually the same day.

Why is there no price list?

Because a figure published before anyone has seen your home is either padded to cover the worst case, or it is about to be revised upward once we walk in. Neither is a good start. A quote written for your house is worth more than a number that was written for nobody.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Recurring visits continue until you tell us otherwise, and you can pause for the summer or stop entirely with a week of notice.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes — everything, including the vacuum. If you would rather we use your products, leave them out and we will. Plenty of clients do, especially with stone counters or a sensitivity to fragrance.
