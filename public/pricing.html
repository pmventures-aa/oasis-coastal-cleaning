Pricing

Quoted for your space

There is no price list on this site, and that is deliberate. Every home is a different size, a different condition and a different rhythm — so every quote is written for the house it is for.

What makes it more

  • More square footage, more bathrooms, more floors
  • The first visit, which catches up on everything
  • Pets, and the hair that comes with them
  • Add-ons — inside the oven, the fridge, the windows
  • A one-time visit rather than a standing one

What makes it less

  • Coming weekly or every two weeks instead of once
  • A home that is already tidy when we arrive
  • Skipping rooms you would rather we left alone
  • Bundling several add-ons into one visit
  • Being on a route we already drive that day

How the rhythm works

The more often, the less each visit

A house cleaned every two weeks never gets far from where we left it, so the visit takes less time — and that saving goes to you. Nothing is locked in: pause for the season or stop with a week of notice.

Weekly

The best rate, and the house never gets far from where we left it.

Every two weeks

The rhythm most family homes settle into.

Monthly

A full reset once a month, with extra time budgeted.

One time

A single visit — a move, a party, or a trial run before you commit.

Tell us about the space and a real number comes back within one business day, usually the same day. There is nothing to pay until you say yes.
