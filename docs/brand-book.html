01

The Brand

Oasis Coastal Cleaning brings cleaning, organizing and laundry to South Florida homes and offices. The name promises relief — a calm, ordered place to land. Every design and copy decision should feel like that promise being kept: warm, unhurried, and visibly professional.

The audience is coastal homeowners, snowbird property owners, short-term rental hosts and small offices across Broward, Palm Beach and Miami-Dade. They are buying trust and disappearance-of-hassle, not the lowest hourly rate. The brand should always look like it costs a little more than the competition — and be worth it.

Tagline (primary)

Fresh Spaces. Happy Places.

Tagline (secondary)

From everyday mess to coastal fresh.

Use the primary on the logo, signage and social. The secondary works well as a website hero line or ad headline because it states the transformation.

02

Logo

The badge is the whole identity — illustration, name, services and tagline in one lockup. Because it carries that much detail, it needs room and size to work.

Clear space: keep empty margin on all sides equal to at least 15% of the badge width. Nothing crosses it — no text, no photo edge, no button.

Minimum size — screen

120 px wide. Below that the services line and tagline turn to mush. For anything smaller, crop to the round illustration alone.

Minimum size — print

1.25 in wide. Business cards, invoice headers, door hangers — do not go under this.

Backgrounds


Cream — preferred

White — fine

Sand — fine

Navy — use sparingly
Do
  • Use the transparent PNG so the badge sits on the page background, not a white square.
  • Scale proportionally — always drag from a corner.
  • Give it a quiet background. Cream or a solid color, never a busy photo.
  • Let the badge be the loudest thing in its area.
Don't
  • Stretch, squash, rotate or tilt it.
  • Recolor it, add a drop shadow, glow or outline.
  • Place it over a photograph or a busy pattern.
  • Pull the illustration apart or use the beach scene on its own.
  • Retype the name next to the badge — the name is already in it.

Used for the word Oasis and nothing else. Never for a sentence, a button, a heading or anything in caps.

05

Voice

Warm, calm, and specific. She is a professional who happens to be friendly — not a friend who happens to clean. Say what gets done, in plain words. Skip exclamation points, skip "we strive to," skip anything a franchise would print.

Sounds right
  • "Every visit ends the same way: floors done last, trash out, doors locked."
  • "Tell us the three rooms that stress you out. We start there."
  • "Laundry picked up Tuesday, folded and back Thursday."
Sounds wrong
  • "We strive to exceed expectations!!"
  • "#1 rated cleaning solutions provider"
  • "Unleash the power of a spotless home"

Service names — always in this order, always these words:

Home · Office · Organizing · Laundry

"Residential" is fine in long-form copy and on the badge, but "Home" is shorter and warmer for navigation, buttons and the services line. Pick one per surface and stay consistent.

Putting it on Cloudflare

#Step
1Point oasiscoastalcleaning.com at Cloudflare — add the site, then change the nameservers at the registrar. Propagation is usually under an hour.
2Create a Cloudflare Pages project and connect it to a Git repo (or drag-and-drop the folder for a first deploy).
3Copy favicon/, logo/, social/, tokens.css and site.webmanifest into the site root so the paths in the head snippet resolve.
4Paste the head snippet into every page's <head>.
5Add the custom domain in Pages, plus the www variant, and let Cloudflare issue the certificate.
6Turn on Always Use HTTPS, Auto Minify and Brotli. Set Browser Cache TTL long — the logo files never change.
7Add an email route so info@oasiscoastalcleaning.com forwards to her inbox, and verify SPF/DKIM before sending anything.
